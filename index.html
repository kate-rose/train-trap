The train trap is a fixed camera watching the river line. It photographs current conditions and whatever rolls past, including the good paint.

Every passing train is also recorded, and clips are kept on a trailing weekly basis. If a train is later tied to a fire start along the line, the recordings are available to investigators on request. Audio is next. IR after that.
