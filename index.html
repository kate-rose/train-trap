The train trap is a pair of fixed cameras watching the river line: one wide on the water, one down at track level. They photograph current conditions and whatever rolls past, including the good paint.

Every passing train is also recorded, and clips are kept on a trailing weekly basis. If a train is alleged or suspected to be involved in a fire, we can provide video on request. Audio is next. IR after that.
